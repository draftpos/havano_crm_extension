---

## 📌 Overview

**Havano CRM Extension** adds critical data fields, activity deadline computation methods, and product interest associations to Odoo CRM leads. It facilitates high-fidelity legacy system migrations while extending standard CRM pipelines with structured contact, business type, and demo management fields.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **Product Interest Association**: Integrates `crm.product` model with search, auto-creation, and validation.
- **Activity Deadline Computations**: Robust calculation of active and overdue lead activity deadlines with custom search domains.
- **Enhanced Contact & Business Fields**: Captures technician assignments, demo types, quote dates, and proposal states.
- **Unified 3-Column Layout**: Structured presentation of contact, deal, and address information.
